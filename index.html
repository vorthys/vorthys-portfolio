How a project runs

Typically 5—10 working days
1

Brief, in writing

What the page must achieve, who reads it, what you already have. Half an hour of questions saves a week of revisions.

2

Direction first

You see the hero and one section before the rest exists. We agree on type, colour and tone there — not at the end.

3

Build and review

Full page on a preview link, tested on phone and desktop. Two rounds of changes are part of the price.

4

Handover

Deployed to your hosting or mine, source files yours, plus a short note on how to edit the parts you'll want to change.
