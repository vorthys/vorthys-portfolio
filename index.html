How the studio works

Three roles, one table

Design

Every project starts from its own subject, not from a template. That's why the roastery, the photo studio and the gym on this page share no visual DNA.

Front-end

Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, or React with Tailwind when the project needs it. No page builders, no plugin stacks, nothing to slow the page down.

Copy

We write the text with the design, in English or Czech. A page that says the right thing in the right place converts better than a prettier page that mumbles.

Small studio means you talk to the people doing the work. No account manager relaying your comments, no ticket queue — you write, the person who built the page answers.

Questions we actually get

Before you write
What makes the price go up?

Three things, in order: how much text has to be written from scratch, how many pages share a layout, and whether the site has to talk to another system — a booking calendar, a payment gateway, a CRM. Design complexity matters far less than people expect, and it never changes the agreed price mid-project.

What if the direction isn't what I imagined?

You see the first screen on day one, before the rest exists. If the direction is wrong we change it there, in a couple of hours. Rebuilding a finished page is what gets expensive — that's exactly why we show the direction first.

Who pays for hosting and the domain?

You do, directly, and the accounts stay in your name. A static site like the demos here can be hosted free, and a domain runs about €10 a year. We'd rather you keep control than have to ask us for access later.

Can I edit the text myself afterwards?

Yes. On handover you get a short note pointing at exactly the lines to change for prices, opening hours or photos. If you'd rather not touch it at all, the care plan covers small edits every month.

Do you build e-shops or WordPress sites?

Not our strong suit, and we'd rather say so. We build fast presentation sites, landing pages and dashboards. For a shop with stock and invoicing, Shopify plus a specialist beats anything we'd hand-code — we can point you to one.

How does payment work?

Half at the start, half on handover, invoiced from Czechia with the right VAT treatment for EU companies. No monthly fee unless you take the care plan.
