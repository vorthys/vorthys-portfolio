How it works

Three steps, none of them a spreadsheet

Hodina sits between your calendar and your accountant. You keep working the way you already do.

Step one

Track by project

Start a timer, or drop a block onto the day afterwards. Rates live on the project, so you never retype them.

Step two

Pick what's billable

Admin and breaks stay off the invoice unless you say otherwise. Every block keeps its own note for the line item.

Step three

Send, then forget

Hodina fills in the VAT treatment from the client's country and VAT number, numbers the invoice, and emails it.

EU VAT

The part everyone gets wrong

Cross-border invoicing has three cases and one of them costs you money if you miss it. Hodina picks the case from the client record.

EU company with VAT ID

Reverse charge, zero VAT on the invoice, the Art. 196 note printed automatically. The client accounts for it at home.

Local client

Domestic rate applies. If you're not registered, the invoice says so instead of quietly charging nothing.

Outside the EU

Out of scope, with the place-of-supply note your accountant expects to see on the document.

Czech specifics

Tracks the turnover threshold and flags the month you cross it, so registration isn't a surprise in April.

FAQ

Questions we actually get

Does this replace my accountant?

No. It gives your accountant documents that don't need fixing — correct numbering, correct VAT note, one export per quarter.

What if I forget to track a day?

Add blocks after the fact. Hodina reads your calendar and suggests them, and you keep or drop each one.

Can I invoice in CZK and get paid in EUR?

Yes. The invoice carries both amounts and the ČNB rate for the date of supply, which is what the tax office asks for.

What happens to my data if I leave?

Export everything as CSV and PDF on the way out. No lock-in period, cancel from the billing page.
